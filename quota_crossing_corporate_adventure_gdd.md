# Quota Crossing: The Corporate Adventure
## Master Game Design Document (v3.0 - The Full Ecosystem)

**Core Hook:** An open-office life-sim RPG where you must survive the ecosystem of a massive B2B tech reseller. You will hunt leads, farm your CRM, craft quotes, battle executives, accessorize your avatar to the nines, and share your glorious (or tragic) sales metrics with friends.

**Visual Style:** Bright, flat-vector graphics, thick outlines, bouncy 60fps animations. Clean, minimalist UI that looks like a friendly, pastel iOS app. Absolutely no pixel art.

---

## 1. "The Badging Process" (Character Creation)
Before stepping onto the OmniTech campus on Day 1, players must visit HR to get their Corporate ID Badge. This is a deep, highly expressive character creator focusing on corporate aesthetics and moods. The avatar is a 2D vector paper-doll that visually updates in real-time.

* **Body & Identity:** Fully gender-neutral sliders for height, build, skin tone, and hair.
* **The "Corporate Vibe" Sliders:**
  * **Posture:** From "Eager Intern (Upright)" to "Tenured Slouch."
  * **Eye Bags:** A slider that adds progressively darker purple circles under the eyes.
  * **The Corporate Smile:** Choose between "Genuine Joy," "Customer Service Smirk," or "Dead Inside Stare."
* **Starter Wardrobe:** Choose your origin style:
  * *The Overdressed Rookie:* A suit that's slightly too big.
  * *The Tech Bro:* Plaid shirt, standard fleece.
  * *The Minimalist:* Black turtleneck.
* **The ID Badge:** The final step generates a physical-looking "OTS ID Badge" on screen, complete with your photo and a randomly generated, hilariously mundane corporate title (e.g., *Junior Synergy Facilitator* or *Associate Spreadsheet Wizard*).

## 2. Wardrobe, Accessories, & The Swag Economy
Throughout the game, your avatar is highly visible. You can customize them constantly by acquiring Wearables. Wearables fall into two categories: **Cosmetic** (pure aesthetic clout) and **Power-Ups** (stat boosters).

### How to Acquire Gear:
1. **The Swag Vending Machine (Gacha):** Spend SPIFF Coins earned from vendor side-quests to roll for random partner-branded gear.
2. **The Corporate Outfitter (Shop):** Spend Commish-Cash at the lobby store for high-end fashion.
3. **Achievement Unlocks (Boss Drops):** Earned exclusively by completing major milestones or defeating C-Suite bosses.

### Accessory Categories & Examples:
* **Headwear & Eyewear:**
  * *Blue Light Glasses (Power-Up):* Reduces Bandwidth (stamina) drain by 15% when doing the Q-Bot 3000 Quote Crafting minigame.
  * *Mirrored Aviators (Cosmetic):* Just makes you look like a hotshot.
  * *Noise-Canceling Headphones (Power-Up):* Renders you immune to "Office Gossip" debuffs while walking through the Bullpen.
* **Torso (The Vest Meta):**
  * *Patagonia-Parody VC Fleece (Power-Up):* Grants +15 Charisma in the "C-Suite Skybridge" biome, lowering Boss starting HP.
  * *Mustard-Stained Tie (Debuff/Joke):* Lowers your Rep but increases your relatable charm with IT Admin NPCs.
* **Lanyards & Pins (The Ultimate Flex):** Tiny, highly collectible flair that shows off your tenure. Getting the "5-Year Diamond Lanyard" requires real in-game grinding and shines with a dynamic iOS glare animation as you walk.

## 3. The "Digital Business Card" (Social Sharing System)
To tap into the Tamagotchi and mobile gaming social virality, players can access their "QBR" (Quarterly Brag Report) at any time. This acts as a visually stunning exportable save-state.

* **The Mechanic:** By tapping their ID Badge in the menu, the screen transitions into an incredibly flashy, trading-card-style Digital Business Card.
* **What it Displays:**
  * Your customized Avatar, animated and wearing all your rarest, flashiest gear.
  * Your Lifetime Commish-Cash Earned.
  * Your Win/Loss Ratio against Prospects.
  * Your Current Corporate Title.
  * Your currently equipped "Desk Plant" status (Alive, Thriving, or Tragically Dead).
* **Foil & Rarity Upgrades:** As you hit quota milestones, your card's background upgrades automatically. It starts as Matte Cardstock, upgrades to Glossy, then Holographic Foil, and finally a Diamond-encrusted looping animation.
* **Export & Flex:** A native iOS ShareLink button allows the player to export this card as a high-resolution, perfectly cropped Image. They can instantly text it to friends, drop it in a Slack/Teams channel, or post it online to boast about their legendary loadout and ridiculous lifetime stats.
* **The Rolodex (Asynchronous Multiplayer):** The exported card includes a tiny QR code / "Friend String." If your friend inputs your string into their game, an NPC version of your avatar will permanently wander their game's Breakroom, wearing your exact outfit.

## 4. The Economy of Stress: Time, Resources, & Currencies
* **The Fiscal Calendar:** 1 real-world minute = 1 in-game hour. The year is divided into 4 Fiscal Quarters. Q4 triggers "The EOQ Blizzard" (frantic music, 1.5x commission multiplier, double-HP bosses).
* **Stats:**
  * *Bandwidth* (Stamina)
  * *Patience* (HP in battle)
  * *Jargon* (MP/Mana)
  * *Rep* (Level)
* **Currencies:**
  * *Base Pay:* Survival money
  * *Commish-Cash:* Wealth / Real Estate / High-End Clothes
  * *SPIFF Coins:* Gacha / Swag currency

## 5. The Core Loop: The 5-Phase Sales Funnel
1. **Prospecting (Gathering):** Equip a "WiFi Scanner" in the 'Networking Lounge' biome. Play a Minesweeper-style minigame to dodge Gatekeepers and dig up a Lead.
2. **Pipeline Management (Farming):** Plant leads in your Pipe-Dex (CRM). Return daily to "Follow Up" (water them). Ignore them, and they rot into "Closed-Lost" weeds.
3. **The Q-Bot 3000 (Crafting):** A grid-based puzzle. Snap server SKUs and software licenses together. Mix incompatible parts, and the bot explodes. Output = A "Quote" weapon.
4. **The Sales Encounter (Turn-Based Combat):** Find the prospect in the wild. Engage in a Pokémon-style battle using Jargon attacks (e.g., Cloud beats Hardware). Use combos like *Move the Needle* + *Boil the Ocean* = *Synergy Blast*. Reduce their Skepticism to 0 and throw an E-Sign Sphere to catch the deal.
5. **The Bid Desk Dungeon (Roguelite):** If your quote is low-margin, navigate a stealth maze in the basement to dodge "Finance Trolls." Get caught, and your payout is slashed.

## 6. Office Base Building & Feng Shui
Your cubicle is your sanctuary. Use an isometric grid to place items bought with Commish-Cash.
* **Upgrades:** Gray Folding Table -> 3x3 Cubicle -> Private Office.
* **Functional Decor:** Ergonomic Chairs boost Max Patience. Espresso Machines generate daily Bandwidth potions.
* **Social Link Visits:** If your desk has a high "Feng Shui" score, vendor NPCs like Tim (Pear Inc.) or Chuck (Frisco) will visit your desk and drop off rare SPIFF Coins or exclusive accessories.

---

## 7. Endgame: The Platinum Trip
The ultimate goal: Hit the $10,000,000 Quota.
* **The Final Boss:** The Grumpy Megacorp CFO. Immune to Jargon. Defeated only with pure math and flawless quote-crafting.
* **The Reward:** You board a private jet to "Isla de Sinergia."
* **New Game+:** A new island map unlocks. No combat. You wear Hawaiian shirts, drink coconuts, and design a beachside executive suite. Back at HQ, your quota doubles, but your Digital Business Card now features a permanent, un-removable pair of VIP Aviator Sunglasses.
